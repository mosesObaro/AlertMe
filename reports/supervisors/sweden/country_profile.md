## 5. Major Doctoral Funding Schemes & Scholarships
### Swedish Salaried Doctoral Position (Doktorandanställning)
* **Provider / University:** Swedish State University System & Swedish Higher Education Ordinance (Högskoleförordningen) (All Swedish Public Universities (KTH, Chalmers, Lund, Uppsala, Umeå, Linköping))
* **Funding Type:** **FULLY_FUNDED**
* **Tuition Coverage:** Full 100% free tuition for all doctoral students (tuition fees are legally waived by Swedish law for PhD researchers)
* **Stipend / Salary:** 31,000 - 35,500 SEK/month gross employment salary (regulated by collective bargaining agreements) + full statutory pension, paid vacation (28 days/yr), healthcare, and parental leave benefits
* **International Eligibility:** ELIGIBLE

* **Application Deadline:** 2026-12-01
