## 5. Major Doctoral Funding Schemes & Scholarships
### TV-L E13 Research Associate (Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter / Doctoral Position)
* **Provider / University:** German Federal States Public Tariff (Tarifvertrag für den öffentlichen Dienst der Länder) (All German Public Universities (TUM, RWTH Aachen, TU Berlin, KIT, TU Dresden, etc.))
* **Funding Type:** **FULLY_FUNDED**
* **Tuition Coverage:** Full tuition-free education (German public universities charge zero tuition; only nominal semester fee ~€150-€350 including transit pass)
* **Stipend / Salary:** €48,000 - €56,000 gross/year (100% TV-L E13 salary) or €36,000 - €42,000 (75% TV-L E13), including full health, unemployment, and statutory pension insurance
* **International Eligibility:** ELIGIBLE
* **Dependant Support:** EXCELLENT
* **Application Deadline:** 2026-12-01
* **Official Source:** [https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-training/studies-in-germany/phd](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-training/studies-in-germany/phd)

### DAAD Doctoral Research Grants (One-Year Grants / Complete Doctorate)
* **Provider / University:** German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) (All Accredited German Universities / Max Planck / Helmholtz Institutes)
* **Funding Type:** **FULLY_FUNDED**
* **Tuition Coverage:** Zero tuition at German universities; DAAD covers all mandatory semester contributions
* **Stipend / Salary:** €1,350/month tax-free stipend + €460/year research allowance + monthly spouse/child supplement (€276/mo spouse + €204/mo child) + comprehensive health insurance
* **International Eligibility:** ELIGIBLE
* **Dependant Support:** EXCELLENT
* **Application Deadline:** 2026-11-01
* **Official Source:** [https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/)
