## 6. Recent Publications
### Proactive Computation Offloading for Connected Autonomous Vehicles in 5G V2X Networks (2023)
* **Authors:** Jianping Wang, Shengli Gao, Zhipeng Liu
* **Venue:** IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems
* **Reference / DOI:** [https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2023.3279182](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITS.2023.3279182)
* **Research Problem:** High-velocity vehicles experience sudden handover disconnections from roadside edge units (RSUs).
* **Approach / Methodology:** Spatial-temporal trajectory forecasting to pre-migrate execution states before vehicle arrives in next cell.
